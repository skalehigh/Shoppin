Shipping,
explained clearly.

Carrier availability, item eligibility, dimensions, destination, and customs rules shape every shipment.

Last updated

22 August 2026

Shipping coordination

Shoppin may coordinate packing and domestic or international shipment through available third-party carriers. Carrier selection depends on the item, origin, destination, serviceability, expected timing, and quoted cost.

Eligibility and prohibited items

Every shipment is subject to carrier and legal restrictions. Regulated medicines, perishables, liquids, batteries, hazardous goods, valuables, plants, food, cosmetics, and other categories may require documentation, special handling, or may be prohibited.

Chargeable weight

Shipping may be charged using the higher of actual weight and volumetric weight after packing. Final dimensions and carrier assessment can therefore change an early estimate.

Customs, duties, and taxes

The recipient may be required to provide information, complete clearance, or pay duties, taxes, storage, inspection, brokerage, or other destination charges. Shoppin cannot guarantee customs release or determine every destination charge in advance.

Timing and tracking

Timelines are estimates unless the carrier explicitly guarantees a service. Customs, weather, service disruptions, incorrect addresses, recipient availability, and regulatory checks may cause delays. Available tracking information is shared after dispatch.

Damage, shortage, or loss

Report visible packaging issues promptly and retain packaging, photos, receipts, and carrier records. Claims are subject to carrier terms, declared value, evidence, filing deadlines, and available insurance.

Questions before shipping

Send the item description, quantity, approximate dimensions or weight, origin, and destination through the Contact page for an eligibility discussion.
