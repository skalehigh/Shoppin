Our beginning

A simple idea: put a helpful person where the customer cannot be.

Why Shoppin began

In 2023, founders P Shashank Veer and Nishant Parepalli recognised how difficult it can be to shop from a physical store when time, distance, limited information, or cross-border access gets in the way. Shoppin was created around dedicated human assistance: visit, show, recommend, coordinate, and deliver.

How the idea expanded

The operating model grew beyond personal shopping to include practical personal assistance, sourcing and export support, and tailored corporate requirements.

What the service believes

Visibility before important decisionsClear scope and cost confirmationHuman communication rather than anonymous ticketsHonest boundaries when a request is not feasible
