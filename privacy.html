Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

Shoppin is operated by Thinkorporation Tech Pvt Ltd. This notice explains how information is handled when you visit this website or choose to contact the Shoppin team through WhatsApp.

Website analytics

We use Plausible Analytics to understand aggregate website usage, such as page visits, referral sources, countries, device types, and which Shoppin call-to-action was selected. Plausible is configured as a privacy-focused analytics service and does not use cookies or create cross-site advertising profiles. We do not send your WhatsApp message text or the contents of a prefilled message to Plausible.

Contacting us through WhatsApp

When you select a WhatsApp button, this website asks for your name, phone number, and an optional email address. The website adds those details to the prefilled WhatsApp message without storing them. You can review the message before sending it through WhatsApp, a service operated by WhatsApp and Meta. Information you choose to send—including your contact details, product requirements, photos, documents, delivery details, and conversation history—will be processed according to WhatsApp's terms and privacy practices as well as this notice.

How we use information you share

  • To understand, quote, coordinate, and fulfil your shopping or assistance request.
  • To communicate approvals, payments, receipts, packing, shipping, and service updates.
  • To meet legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, customs, and operational obligations.
  • To improve Shoppin's services and customer experience.

Service providers and international transfers

We may share necessary information with retailers, shoppers, logistics providers, payment providers, customs or professional advisers when required to fulfil your request. These providers may process information in India or other countries relevant to your transaction. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably required for the service, legal, accounting, dispute-resolution, and fraud-prevention purposes. We apply reasonable safeguards, but no internet or messaging service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete information held by Shoppin, subject to legal and operational retention requirements. You can also choose not to send sensitive information through WhatsApp and ask the team for an appropriate alternative.
