Shipping calculations

Actual, volumetric, and chargeable weight.

Actual weight

The measured physical weight of the packed shipment.

Volumetric weight

A carrier calculation based on package dimensions. It reflects the space a parcel occupies, not only what it weighs.

Chargeable weight

Carriers generally charge using whichever is higher: actual weight or volumetric weight. The applicable carrier formula and quote are confirmed for the shipment.

Before you approve

Ask for a clear breakdown of the known costs and which amounts remain estimates or depend on third-party assessment. Customs duties and destination taxes may not be known in advance.
