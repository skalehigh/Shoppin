Getting started

Using Shoppin

What should I send in my first message?+

Send a product link, photo, store name, city, task description, budget range, destination, quantity, or deadline. A rough description is enough to start.

When is WhatsApp support available?+

The currently published chat hours are Monday to Sunday, 8:00 AM to 12:00 AM IST. Urgent requests can also be discussed by calling +91 88856 29392.

When can in-store shopping be scheduled?+

The currently published shopping window is Monday to Sunday, 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM IST, subject to the operating hours and availability of the relevant store.

Can I join a store visit by video call?+

For suitable personal-shopping requests, a shopper can video call you from the store so you can compare available options and inspect visible details before approval.

Can Shoppin buy from websites in other countries?+

Yes. Share the product link, country, and destination. The team can discuss purchase coordination, receiving, visible checks, consolidation, and eligible onward shipping where supported.

Which cities and countries are supported?+

Coverage depends on the request, shopper or provider availability, item, and destination. Share the locations and the team will confirm feasibility.

How are charges calculated?+

Charges may include the agreed service work, product cost, local travel, third-party services, packing, payment charges, and shipping. The known scope and expected costs are confirmed before work begins.

When do I approve the purchase?+

For assisted shopping, the selected product and expected final cost should be confirmed with you before purchase. Availability and prices may change at the retailer.

Can several purchases be combined?+

Eligible purchases may be coordinated and consolidated. Storage, packaging, prohibited-item rules, and shipping suitability must be confirmed for the specific items.

What is the virtual address or lockbox?+

It is a designated Indian receiving address for eligible online purchases. Current published pricing includes up to 30 days of storage, starting when the first order arrives, followed by a ₹49 per-day charge.

Can you ship internationally?+

Worldwide shipping can be coordinated for eligible items and serviceable destinations. Carrier acceptance, documentation, customs rules, duties, and destination restrictions apply.

Who pays duties and destination taxes?+

Customs duties, taxes, and clearance charges depend on the destination and shipment. Unless explicitly agreed otherwise, these may be payable by the recipient.

Can I return an item?+

Returns depend on the original retailer's policy, product condition, timing, location, and the practicality of returning it. International shipping costs and duties may not be recoverable.

What products cannot be shipped?+

Carriers and customs authorities restrict or prohibit various goods. Medicines, liquids, perishables, batteries, hazardous materials, valuables, and other regulated items require specific confirmation.

Is WhatsApp the only contact option?+

WhatsApp is the fastest way to begin. You can also email or call using the verified details on the Contact page.
