Frequently asked

Everything you need to know.

Can I join the store visit on a video call?+

Yes. For suitable shopping requests, your shopper can video call you from the store so you can compare options and inspect details before approving a purchase.

Can you shop from websites in other countries?+

Yes. Share the product link, country, and delivery destination. The team will confirm whether purchase assistance, receiving, checks, and onward shipping are feasible.

How are the charges calculated?+

Charges depend on the service, location, time, purchase requirements, packing, and shipping. The scope and expected cost are confirmed with you before work begins.

Can purchases from several stores be combined?+

Eligible purchases can be coordinated and consolidated before shipping. The team will confirm storage, packing, and dispatch options for your specific request.

Can you ship internationally?+

Shoppin can coordinate worldwide shipping for eligible items. Carrier availability, chargeable weight, customs requirements, and destination restrictions apply.

What should I send in my first WhatsApp message?+

A photo, link, store name, city, budget, deadline, or a simple description is enough. The team will ask the right follow-up questions.
