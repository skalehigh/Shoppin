Retailer return policy

Products purchased through Shoppin remain subject to the original seller's return, exchange, warranty, and refund rules. Some sellers, sale items, food, cosmetics, jewellery, custom products, and other categories may not allow returns.

Raise the issue promptly

Contact Shoppin as soon as possible with the order context, issue description, photos, packaging, labels, and receipt information. Delays may make the retailer's return window unavailable.

Service fees and incurred costs

Completed store visits, assistance, sourcing work, local travel, payment charges, customisation, packing, shipping, duties, and other costs already incurred may not be refundable even when a retailer accepts the product return.

International returns

Returning an internationally shipped item may require additional courier charges, customs documentation, duties, and time. These costs can exceed the product value and are assessed case by case.

Damaged or incorrect items

Retain all packaging and evidence. Resolution depends on whether the issue arose with the retailer, during handling, or with the carrier and remains subject to the relevant third-party terms.

Request support

Use the Contact page and provide the purchase details so the team can review the available options.
